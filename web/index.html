    Recursive self-improvement

    Each finished prompt becomes a published model, and later prompts start from what earlier ones learned. Every row is one prompt, timed from typing to a published model, with success measured on 20 held-out scenes; "Started from" shows what it built on.     Model library

    Every model here is a custom-trained vision-language-action policy: a frame from its own rollout, its success on held-out scenes, and how long it took to train. Click one to watch it run, test it on new scenes, remix it with a new prompt, or train a new one.

    What should the arm learn?

    Describe a task. Dojo turns it into a success check, collects demos, fine-tunes a vision-language-action policy and measures it on 20 scenes it never saw.

    Pick the robot

    Choose an arm, a wrist and an end effector. Every part is a real open-source model with its licence and source. Dojo assembles them into one MuJoCo file, checks it, and trains the next model on it.
